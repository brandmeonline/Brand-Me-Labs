# Delivery roadmap and proposed work packages

[Audit home](README.md) · [Branches and preservation](BRANCHES_CODE.md) · [Integration setup](INTEGRATIONS_INFRASTRUCTURE.md) · [Findings](evidence/findings.json)

The fastest credible result is a **single-revision wardrobe-and-friends pilot**: a member signs in, explicitly saves guest preferences, adds a self-reported garment through the browser, sees it after relogin, asks selected friends, receives a persisted decision/notification and exactly one eligible reward, then signs out with accurate device-data controls. This uses existing persona, wardrobe, social, identity-store and event code. Retail checkout, live AI, token transfers and realistic fit claims are separate milestones; they do not need to block this first coherent outcome.

First establish that journey with synthetic development identity in a disposable stack (**M0**). A pilot with real users (**M1**) additionally requires production browser identity, scoped privacy controls, operational gates and authorized staging resources. M0 is not a substitute for M1. Estimates below are engineering ranges, not deadlines or cost quotations. They assume one engineer familiar with the stack plus review, working access to a disposable runtime, no undiscovered major schema rewrite and no provider-approval delay. Overlap is substantial; do not add the ranges into a promised delivery date.
